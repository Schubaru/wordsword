wordsword

A dictionary that keeps your words. Look up a word while you read and get it in plain English. Then it's saved, so you can come back to it next week.

  • Plain-English definitions, synonyms and pronunciation
  • Every word you look up is saved, and you can sort words into wordlists
  • Flashcards that space out your reviews
  • Four word games: a daily haiku with a missing word (Haikuzy), a daily five-letter word (Plexicon), a daily Linking words puzzle, and Word Wheel
  • No ads, no tracking. Your words stay on your iPhone
