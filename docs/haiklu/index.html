    How to play

    One word is missing from today's haiku. Guess it in four tries.

    Every guess scores 1 to 100 for how close it is in meaning. The word itself scores 100.

    Solve it in fewer guesses for a higher total.

    Only real words count, and a typo gets fixed for you.

    A new haiku every midnight.
