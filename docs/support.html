Support

Something not working, or have an idea? Email abschuzy@gmail.com, and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something broke. You'll hear back within a few days.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. Signing in with Apple only sets a username.

Where are my words stored?

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. wordsword has no server, so there's no backup or sync between devices yet. If you delete the app, your words are deleted too.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → You → Delete account. This removes your account from your iPhone. Your saved words stay. To remove wordsword from your Apple ID too: iOS Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → wordsword.

How do I delete my words?

Settings → Clear all my words removes every word and wordlist. To remove a single word, swipe it in the Library.

A word won't define

wordsword needs an internet connection to look up a word for the first time. Words already in your library work offline. If a lookup fails while you're online, the dictionary service may be busy, so try again in a minute.

The mic doesn't work

Check iOS Settings → wordsword, and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition.

"Explain it differently" repeats the dictionary

New explanations are written on your iPhone by Apple's on-device model, which needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on. On other iPhones, wordsword shows the dictionary's own wording.

When are the next daily puzzles?

Haikuzy, Plexicon and Linking words each get a new puzzle every day at midnight, your local time.
